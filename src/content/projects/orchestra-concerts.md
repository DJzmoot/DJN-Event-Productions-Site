Lighting an orchestra is about restraint: the musicians need clean, even light to read their music and see the conductor, and the audience needs to see every section clearly.

At the Suffolk Theater, the drape behind the players was striped in blue and amber to give the stage depth and warmth, while the orchestra, conductor and featured vocalist sat in an even, unfussy wash that let the music lead.
