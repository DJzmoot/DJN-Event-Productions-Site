Architectural lighting treats the site itself as part of the design. Trees, façades and landmark features become a canvas, so the space reads as an event the moment guests arrive.

Here, weather-rated fixtures at the base of each tree washed the trunks and canopies in deep red, giving the open outdoor site a strong, cohesive look after dark. The same approach applies to a historic landmark at the [Huntington Lighthouse Festival](/work/huntington-lighthouse-festival/).
