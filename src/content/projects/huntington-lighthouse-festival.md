The Huntington Lighthouse sits on its own rock foundation in the bay, which makes every part of a production there — load-in, power, audio and lighting — a waterfront logistics exercise.

After dark, the lighthouse itself became the centrepiece: its stone façade washed in textured colour and its lantern gallery picked out in light, mirrored in the water around it.
