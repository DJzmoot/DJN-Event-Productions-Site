The Friends of the School of Architecture & Design gala for the New York Institute of Technology was produced by National Event Connection (NEC), with Nick as part of the technical team.

An LED video wall anchored the stage, carrying the event's branded graphics, with a matching podium display for speakers. Green uplighting on the ballroom's columns and ceiling coves extended the event's colour from the screen into the room, and a single control position ran audio, video switching and presentation playback through the evening.
