The Suffolk Theater is a restored 1930s Art Deco venue in Riverhead, where the ornate proscenium and drape are part of every show.

For band concerts there, the lighting frames the stage with moving lights on an overhead truss, on booms either side and on the floor, layering deep blue washes and amber beams, or saturated green, blue and magenta, through haze, so the room feels like a concert from the moment the audience takes their seats.
