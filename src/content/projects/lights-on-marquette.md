Lights on Marquette is a residential holiday light show built around more than 10,000 individually addressable pixels, choreographed in xLights and synchronised to music.

Beyond the sequencing, the project covered the practical engineering that keeps a show like this running night after night: controller layout, power distribution and a weather-ready outdoor installation.
