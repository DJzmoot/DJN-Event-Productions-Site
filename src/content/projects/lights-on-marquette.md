Lights on Marquette is a residential animated light show built around more than 10,000 individually addressable pixels, choreographed in xLights and synchronised to music.

The show changes with the season. For Halloween, the rooflines run in purple and green, with animated pixel props, skeletons and uplit pillars along the fence. For Christmas, the same pixel mega-tree, arches and outlines carry a full holiday show.

Beyond the sequencing, the project covers the practical engineering that keeps a show like this running night after night: controller layout, power distribution and a weather-ready outdoor installation.
