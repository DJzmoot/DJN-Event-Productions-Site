Relay For Life is an overnight community fundraiser. At LIU Post it filled a full university gymnasium — a stage and inflatable arch at one end, a walking track and activity areas across the floor, and bleachers packed with students.

The lighting turned a bright, functional athletic space into an evening environment: saturated colour across the ceiling and bleachers, truss-mounted moving lights for the stage and performances, and dedicated looks for speakers, ceremonies and the luminaria walk.
