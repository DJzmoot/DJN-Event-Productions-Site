An LED video wall looks effortless from the audience, and the work behind it is what makes it so: panels assembled and locked true, data and power run cleanly, processing configured, and content prepared to fit the wall's resolution and proportions.

The build shown here became the main screen at the [NYIT Friends Gala](/work/nyit-friends-gala/), produced by National Event Connection (NEC). Content was curated and prepared for the wall and the matching podium display, and the video was run live through the event.
