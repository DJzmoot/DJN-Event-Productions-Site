The Long Island Aquarium makes an unusual reception venue: guests dine among the tanks, beneath a stone balcony and beside murals of the sea.

The lighting leaned into that setting. Cool blue uplighting across the walls, columns and arches extends the underwater feel of the tanks into the room, while the tables stay in warm candlelight, so the space reads as both immersive and intimate.
