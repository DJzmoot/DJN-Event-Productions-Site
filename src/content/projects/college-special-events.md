College special events often happen in the hardest rooms to light: gymnasiums and athletic centres with high ceilings, hard surfaces and bright overhead fixtures, which need to feel like an event space for one evening.

Ground-supported truss towers carry moving lights either side of the performance position, while programmed colour washes across the ceiling trusses change the scale and mood of the whole room. The result turns a familiar campus space into somewhere that feels like a show, then returns it to the athletic schedule the next morning.
